turnstilePatch — CDP MouseEvent.screenX / screenY patcher

Upstream technique:
  https://github.com/TheFalloutOf76/CDP-bug-MouseEvent-.screenX-.screenY-patcher
  https://github.com/Xewdy444/CDP-bug-MouseEvent-.screenX-.screenY-patcher

Vendored into this package from the Cursor-Register research copy
(vendor/cursor-register/turnstilePatch), then updated to the getter form
(clientX/clientY + random offset, also PointerEvent) that Turnstile checks.
Manifest shape still matches Cursor-Register MV3 (MAIN world, all_frames,
document_start).

What it does (and does not do):
  Patches CDP's broken screenX/screenY fingerprint. It is NOT a Turnstile
  solver, captcha farm, or checkbox auto-clicker.

Playwright note:
  Chrome extensions do NOT load under Playwright's chrome-headless-shell
  (headless:false / headed is required for --load-extension). In headless
  mode auto-reg still injects script.js via addInitScript.
